# Funnel Preflight — Full Question Bank

Ask from the active gate only. Prefer forced choices. Mark each answer `LOCKED` only when specific enough to build against.

---

## Gate A — Product & Offer

1. What is the **one** primary thing we are selling on this funnel?
   - [ ] Physical book
   - [ ] Digital book / ebook
   - [ ] Online self-paced program
   - [ ] Live / cohort program
   - [ ] Hybrid (specify)
   - [ ] Membership / continuity
   - [ ] Done-with-you / 1:1
   - [ ] Other: ________
2. Working title (exact spelling)?
3. Subtitle or one-line promise?
4. What does the buyer **receive** on day one (files, login, calendar invite, shipped book)?
5. What does success look like 30 / 90 days after purchase (observable outcome)?
6. What are we explicitly **not** selling on this front end?
7. Is this a new offer, a relaunch, or a repackage of something that already exists?
8. If a book: print only, digital only, or bundled? ISBN / already published?
9. If a program: length, session count, live vs recorded, group size, start dates?
10. Who fulfills support questions in the first 14 days?

**Lock test:** A stranger could describe the product, format, and delivery in one breath without asking clarifying questions.

---

## Gate B — Economics & Backend

1. Front-end price? Currency?
2. Payment plan? Amounts and number of payments?
3. Checkout order bump? What + price? (Yes requires a one-sentence bump pitch.)
4. Upsell 1 (OTO1) after purchase? What + price? Or **No upsell**.
5. Upsell 2? What + price? Or **No**.
6. Downsell after declining an upsell? What + price? Or **No downsell**.
7. Guarantee / refund window and conditions?
8. Expected or historical conversion assumptions (opt-in %, sales %, AOV) — even rough?
9. Maximum allowable cost per purchase / per lead?
10. Tax, shipping, or international constraints?
11. Affiliate / partner cut on this offer?

**Lock test:** Checkout and OTO pages could be built tomorrow without a pricing meeting.

---

## Gate C — Avatar & Promise

1. Who is the single primary buyer (role, industry, life stage)?
2. What are they struggling with **in their words**?
3. What have they already tried that failed?
4. What do they want that they would pay for now?
5. What belief must shift before they buy?
6. Mechanism name (your unique approach / named system)?
7. Proof on hand today: testimonials, case results, credentials, media?
8. Proof still needed before launch?
9. Cold traffic awareness: Unaware / Problem-aware / Solution-aware / Product-aware / Most aware?
10. Disqualifiers (who should bounce)?

**Lock test:** Ad headline and VSL Snap Suggestion could be written from this section alone.

---

## Gate D — Brand & Creative System

1. Brand system for this funnel?
   - [ ] Wedge colors
   - [ ] Bignition colors
   - [ ] Other kit (name + where hex values live)
2. Confirm primary, secondary, accent, background, text colors (paste hex).
3. Logo / wordmark file path or URL?
4. Typography preferences (or "use kit defaults")?
5. On-camera / voice talent?
6. VSL style: classic Benson slide+VO, talking head, hybrid?
7. Any must-use phrases, legal disclaimers, or claims to avoid?

**Lock test:** A designer could theme ClickFunnels without asking "which brand?"

---

## Gate E — Traffic & Capture Plan

1. Primary paid click channel for launch?
2. Daily/weekly test budget for first traffic test?
3. Organic channels in play: LinkedIn, YouTube, email list, partners, other?
4. Capture method(s):
   - [ ] Quiz funnel (preferred for cold social)
   - [ ] Static lead magnet opt-in
   - [ ] Webinar registration
   - [ ] Straight-to-VSL (warm/hot only — justify)
5. If quiz: 3–5 **result buckets** (name + who it fits + next offer angle) — buckets before questions.
6. Email gate placement: pre-quiz / mid-quiz / result unlock?
7. Other lead magnet besides quiz result? Exact title + format (PDF, video, checklist, audio)?
8. Where do LinkedIn post notes and YouTube descriptions point? (single URL)
9. CRM / email platform / funnel builder of record?
10. Pixel + conversion events defined?

**Lock test:** A media buyer knows the landing URL and success event; a creator knows the CTA URL for notes.

---

## Gate F — Funnel Map & Assets

1. Draw the path: Traffic → _____ → _____ → _____ → Purchase → _____ → Thank-you.
2. List every page URL slug (even draft slugs).
3. Email sequences required: delivery, nurture, cart abandon, post-purchase, segment-by-quiz-bucket?
4. Promo content list: LinkedIn posts (#), YouTube videos (#), ad angles (#).
5. Who writes VSL with Benson structure? Who edits video? Who builds pages?
6. Launch date or "build complete by" date?
7. Definition of done for v1 (what can wait for v2)?

**Lock test:** A project board could be created with no missing swimlanes.

---

## Pressure questions (use when answers stay fuzzy)

- "If we only sold one thing this quarter, is it still this?"
- "Would you buy this tomorrow at this price for this outcome?"
- "What breaks if the upsell is deleted — does the front end still make sense?"
- "Are we generating leads or cash first? Pick one primary job for this funnel."
- "Which brand colors appear on the first pixel the prospect sees?"
