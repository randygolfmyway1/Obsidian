# Marketer Frameworks Behind Funnel Preflight

Use these to explain *why* a gate question matters. Do not lecture; cite a principle in one or two sentences when the user resists getting specific.

## Jon Benson (BNSN) — VSL & funnel copy

Jon Benson popularized the Video Sales Letter and ships frameworks via Now VSL / BNSN-style AI copy systems. Words remain the majority of conversion even inside ClickFunnels-style builders.

### 5-Step VSL Formula (post-preflight only)

1. **Snap Suggestion (Lead)** — Pattern interrupt + compelling promise + curiosity loop. First seconds decide stay-or-bounce.
2. **Story** — Relatable background and vital connection; earn the right to teach.
3. **Problem** — Dimensionalize the big problem, root cause, and cost of inaction (in the prospect's world).
4. **Solution / Mechanism** — Named unique mechanism; disqualify inferior alternatives; open the loop to the product.
5. **Offer / Close** — Product construction, bonuses, price architecture, proof, guarantee, urgency, future pacing, clear CTA.

Related seven-beat teaching sometimes expands Offer into Offer → Action → Close; treat that as elaboration of step 5, not a reason to skip product clarity.

### Benson implications for preflight

- You cannot write Solution/Offer without a **named product**, **mechanism**, and **price**.
- Dual modality (read + hear) is a production choice under Gate D, not a substitute for Gate A.
- Email copy often reuses VSL beats (problem → mechanism → offer). Lock the offer first so sequences do not contradict the page.

## Joe Polish (+ Dean Jackson) — Offer, before-unit, relationships

From I Love Marketing / Genius Network teaching:

### Marketing = selling in advance

Prospects should arrive **pre-interested, pre-motivated, pre-qualified, predisposed**. Traffic and lead magnets do that job; the sales page closes it.

### Right offer beats clever copy

A compelling offer is far more powerful than a clever argument. If Gate A/B are mushy, more headlines will not save the funnel.

### Eight Profit Activators (condensed for funnel design)

1. Select a **single** target market  
2. Compel a response (click, opt-in, apply)  
3. Educate and motivate toward a meeting/decision  
4. Present the offering  
5. Deliver a world-class experience  
6. After-sales service  
7. Nurture lifetime relationships  
8. Orchestrate referrals  

Preflight focuses on 1–4 and fulfillment readiness for 5–6. Do not ignore 7–8 when mapping thank-you and post-purchase email.

### Before / During / After

- **Before:** identify, attract, educate, capture (ads, LinkedIn, YouTube, quiz, lead magnet)  
- **During:** transaction and delivery experience  
- **After:** nurture + referrals (often neglected; still plan thank-you and onboarding)

### Dean Jackson anchors (GET / WANT / BENEFIT / NOW)

Every message should tie to what they get, what they want, the benefit, and why now. Use this when reviewing ad/LinkedIn/YouTube CTAs in Gate E.

### Prospect vending machine vs slot machine

Design for predictable input → output (known avatar, known offer, known capture URL). Slot-machine marketing is random posting without a locked destination.

## Direct-response funnel architecture (Click / quiz / OTO pattern)

Industry-standard path this vault assumes for click campaigns:

**Traffic → Opt-in or Quiz → Result / Thank-you → Bridge or VSL Sales Page → Checkout → Order Bump → Upsell (OTO) → Downsell (only on upsell no) → Thank-you → Email**

Rules of thumb:

- One primary job per funnel (leads **or** front-end cash — pick a primary).
- Downsells appear only after an upsell decline.
- Quiz: define **result buckets first**, then write only questions that change bucket, qualify, or route.
- Social (LinkedIn / YouTube) is traffic; the notes/description CTA must send to the capture URL.
- Map every branch before build day so pages are not orphaned.

## How the skill uses these together

| Phase | Guide |
|-------|--------|
| Clarity & offer | Polish / Jackson — right offer, single market, before-unit |
| Capture & traffic | Quiz/opt-in DR patterns + GET/WANT/BENEFIT/NOW in promo |
| VSL, sales page, email | Benson 5-step after Gates A–C locked |
| Backend revenue | Classic OTO / bump / downsell architecture |
| Lifetime value | Polish after-unit — onboarding, nurture, referrals |

If frameworks conflict in conversation, **offer clarity wins**. Never start Benson copy to paper over an unlocked product.
